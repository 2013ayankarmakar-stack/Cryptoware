# Cryptoware

This is a file encrypting malware sample (AKA Cryptoware). This is just the framework; feel free to tweak things!

Cryptoware (or crypto-ransomware) is a type of malicious software that infects your device and encrypts your personal files, making them inaccessible. The attacker then demands a ransom payment—typically in cryptocurrency—in exchange for the digital key required to unlock your data.

### Popular Examples:

    WannaCry: A massive 2017 global outbreak that infected hundreds of thousands of computers worldwide. It utilized a network worm feature to spread rapidly across organizations, locking systems and demanding Bitcoin payments.
    LockBit: Operating primarily as a Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) model, LockBit affiliates have carried out devastating attacks on large enterprises, government entities, and healthcare organizations globally.
    ALPHV (BlackCat): Known for pioneering "triple extortion," BlackCat attacks not only encrypt local networks and leak stolen data, but also hit victims with Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks to compound the pressure.
    RansomHub: A highly active RaaS group known for targeting critical infrastructure and often "double dipping" by pressuring victims with leaked data after they thought they had resolved the initial breach.
